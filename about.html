About

I am a forward-thinking data scientist with deep specialisation in translational data science, with extensive experience delivering technical solutions and advanced statistical analysis across the pharmaceutical industry.

Currently I leverage complex multi-modal data to develop modular analytical tools and future-ready computational roadmaps that drive exploratory translational analysis at Hoffmann-La Roche and the University of Oxford. I have a proven track record in providing technical leadership for early-phase clinical trial data analysis, standardised programming, and evidence generation based on FAIR principles and ICH-GCP compliance.

Based in Basel, Switzerland. Open to research collaborations.
