Life between
the papers

Photos, places, and moments outside the lab — from Basel's Rhine to Oxford's quads and everywhere in between.

In a few words

Science occupies most of my waking hours, but not all of them. I find that stepping away from the screen — whether on a long walk along the Rhine, exploring a new city, or cooking something elaborate — gives me the perspective to come back to hard problems with fresh eyes.

I grew up in Mumbai, moved to Germany for my masters, then to Switzerland and the UK for my doctoral work. Each place has left something behind. Basel's orderliness, Oxford's depth, Mumbai's energy — they all live somewhere in how I think.
